What to put in the first note.

01

The outcome

What should the organization be able to do, answer, or demonstrate when the work is complete?

02

The constraint

Name the regulation, audit date, security concern, operating limit, or business dependency that cannot be ignored.

03

The current state

Describe the systems, people, documents, and evidence already available, even when the picture is incomplete.

The decision

Identify the choice, approval, or next move that needs a responsible owner and a defensible record.
