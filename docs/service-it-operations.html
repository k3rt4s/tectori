Service line 05

Administration that is repeatable and documented.

For organizations that run on Windows, Active Directory, Azure, IIS, and Microsoft 365 and need administration that can be repeated, explained, and undone.

Who this is for

For environments where changes are made and not recorded.

Administrative work often happens quickly, by hand, and without a record. It works until someone asks what changed, or until a change has to be reversed and nobody knows what it replaced.

This work suits organizations that want routine administration to follow a standard practice, with the record produced as part of doing the work rather than afterward.

What the work includes

A standard practice for making changes.

  • Dry runs before changes
  • Plan records during changes
  • Rollback reporting after changes
  • Automation with safety rails built in
  • Runbooks for routine work
  • Decision records for changes that carry risk

Deliverables

What you receive.

Automation with safety rails

Scripts and jobs that preview what they will do, refuse unsafe input, and report what they changed.

Runbooks

Written procedures for the work that repeats, detailed enough for someone else to run.

Decision records

A short record of what changed, why, who approved it, and how to reverse it.

How it supports audit readiness

Every change produces a plan, a record, and a rollback path.

Every change produces a plan, a record, and a rollback path, which is exactly what an examiner asks to see. Change control stops being a separate exercise and becomes a property of how the work is done.

Tectori's free tools include the same automation practice, published openly rather than described.

What this is not

What this service line does not cover.

  • A help desk or end user support contract.
  • Staff augmentation billed by the hour with no defined outcome.
  • Rewriting every process at once. Work starts with what breaks or repeats most.

Make the record a by-product of the work.
