Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before the first conversation.

Tectori is a founder-led practice for regulated and growing organizations. These answers describe the work, operating model, and technology portfolio as they exist today.

About the practice

Tectori, in practical terms.

What does Tectori do?

Tectori provides founder-led cloud architecture and operations, cybersecurity, compliance and risk management, IT operations, fractional IT and security leadership, and governed AI consulting.

Who does Tectori serve?

Tectori is designed for banks, credit unions, healthcare organizations, regulated businesses, and growing companies that need technology to support customer diligence, enterprise expectations, or regulatory scrutiny.

What is audit-ready IT?

Audit-ready IT connects regulatory and business requirements to architecture decisions, policies, controls, ownership, documentation, testing, and evidence produced during normal operation. It is not a promise that every audit will produce a particular result.

How does Tectori approach AI governance?

Tectori treats AI governance as an operating system with approved use cases, accountable owners, data boundaries, human review, monitoring, incident handling, change control, and retained decision records.

Is Tectori a large consulting firm?

No. Tectori is a founder-led practice. Clients work directly with Jonathan Bowker, the practitioner responsible for recommendations, design, and delivery.

Does Tectori sell software?

Tectori builds and maintains several products as separate brands, including Fortivra, Compliance Compass, MailSweep, FileIQ, Architect Copilot, and Lumenwatch. They are working reference implementations of the standards behind Tectori's consulting work and are not offered for sale at this time. Tectori also publishes free, open source tools on GitHub, including Ops Toolkit, Security Program Templates, and Email Evidence Tools.

Where is Tectori located?

Tectori is based in Nashville, Tennessee, and works remotely with organizations nationwide.

How do I start a conversation with Tectori?

Email contact@tectori.com or call (615) 829‑6802 with the business outcome, technology or compliance constraint, current state, and decision or deadline in view.
