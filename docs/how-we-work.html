How we work

Direct access. Clear ownership. Useful records.

Tectori is a founder-led consultancy. Each engagement provides direct access to the practitioner responsible for the recommendation, the delivery, and the evidence behind it.

Engagement models

Match the working model to the problem.

Fractional leadership

Ongoing fractional CIO or CISO support for priorities, governance, executive communication, vendors, budgets, and program delivery.

Defined projects

A scoped build, migration, control program, automation workflow, or operating improvement with agreed deliverables and decision owners.

Assessments

An objective review of architecture, security, audit readiness, operations, or AI governance with findings and a practical action path.

Working rhythm

Set the expectations before the work starts.

01

Scope

Define the business outcome, system boundary, risks, constraints, deliverables, and work that is outside the engagement.

02

Owners

Name the people who can approve, provide access, accept risk, answer questions, and receive the final work.

03

Cadence

Agree on meeting rhythm, written updates, response expectations, escalation paths, and the decisions that need live discussion.

04

Evidence

Decide what records the work will produce, where they belong, who reviews them, and what must remain confidential.

05

Handoff

Close with current documentation, open risks, operating instructions, verification results, and the next accountable action.

Remote delivery

Based in Nashville. Available nationwide.

Services are delivered remotely nationwide. Scheduling and working hours are agreed for each engagement based on the systems involved, business deadlines, and the people who need to participate.

Flexible delivery does not mean unclear availability. The kickoff sets the communication cadence, expected response windows, decision path, and any production support boundary.

Professional practice

Keep learning tied to real systems.

The practice stays current through certification maintenance, teaching experience, direct technical work, and reference implementations that exercise the same governance and operating principles described throughout this site.

Tectori does not publish broad culture promises. There are no open roles at this time. Hiring information will be published only when a real role and its actual terms exist.

Operating principles

Simple rules for serious work.

Say what is known

Separate verified facts, working assumptions, open questions, and decisions that still need an owner.

Do not hide failure

A failed action is reported as a failure. Partial work says what completed, what did not, and what happens next.

Leave the system understandable

Documentation, ownership, recovery paths, and evidence are deliverables, not cleanup tasks after the implementation.

Bring a real problem. Leave with a clear operating path.
