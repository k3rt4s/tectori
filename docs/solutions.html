Tectori solutions

Small tools built around consequential work.

These products are built and maintained by Tectori's founder as separate product brands and working reference implementations. They demonstrate the build standards behind Tectori's consulting work: governance, safety rails, documentation, and rollback by default. They are not Tectori service lines, and consulting engagements do not depend on them.

Built from real operating needs

Accountability stays visible.

Each solution begins with a repeated problem in technology operations: evidence that is difficult to trace, mail that is expensive to review, files that have lost their context, designs that reach review with unresolved gaps, or administrative work that is difficult to reverse.

The common design rule is straightforward. Automation can prepare, organize, and carry work, but important decisions remain reviewable and owned by a person.

Separate from these, Tectori publishes free, open source tools that anyone can download and use today.

Fortivra

An AI security discovery and reporting toolkit. Authorized, read-only collection identifies AI assets, data paths, identities, permissions, secrets exposure, and monitoring gaps, then produces executive findings, evidence, and a prioritized remediation roadmap.

Designed forSecurity leaders who need evidence-backed visibility into AI systems before an examiner, board, or customer asks for it.

Compliance Compass

An assisted compliance workspace that maps requirements to policies and control frameworks, then identifies gaps and prepares structured review outputs.

Designed forCompliance leaders and operators who need a clearer line from obligation to control, owner, document, and evidence.

MailSweep

An assisted mailbox triage system that classifies messages into practical categories and prepares bulk actions, unsubscribe work, and focused review.

Designed forPeople with overloaded mailboxes who want faster decisions while keeping proposed changes visible before execution.

FileIQ

A local file intelligence engine for indexing, enrichment, deduplication, transcription, classification, and guided renaming across large collections.

Designed forTeams and individuals who need to recover context from accumulated files without sending their content to an unmanaged service.

Architect Copilot

A local design review advisor that indexes diagrams and documentation, identifies gaps, and helps prepare unresolved questions for a person-led architecture review.

Designed forTechnology teams that want more complete review packages and a clearer record of architecture decisions.

Lumenwatch

A local command line tool for lawful public-source personal exposure monitoring. Findings remain under the operator's control rather than entering a hosted dashboard.

Designed forAuthorized personal security reviews where data minimization and local handling are important requirements.
