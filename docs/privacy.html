Privacy policy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is the full list.

Effective date: September 9, 2025

What this website does not collect

The Tectori website is a static informational site. It sets no cookies. It runs no analytics. It uses no tracking scripts and no advertising technology. It loads no fonts, scripts, or other resources from third party services.

The site has no forms that transmit data. The client login page is a design preview. Anything typed into it stays in your browser, is cleared after each attempt, and is never sent to or stored by Tectori.

Hosting logs

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical request data, such as IP addresses, for security and operational purposes. Tectori does not receive or review per visitor logs. GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email you choose to send

If you email contact@tectori.com, Tectori receives your email address and whatever you write. That information is used to respond to you and to keep normal business records. It is not sold. It is not shared for marketing.

Links to other sites

Pages on this site may link to other websites, such as LinkedIn. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Questions about this policy can go to contact@tectori.com.
