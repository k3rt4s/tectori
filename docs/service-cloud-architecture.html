Service line 02

Cloud environments built to be reviewed.

Environments are designed with governance and evidence built in from day one. Controls, records, and recovery are part of the design, not additions made before an audit. Azure is the primary platform.

Who this is for

For environments that have to answer for themselves.

This work suits organizations building a cloud environment for the first time, migrating from on premises systems, or improving an environment that grew without a governance baseline.

The shared symptom is an environment that works but cannot explain itself. Resources exist that nobody can account for, access was granted for a reason nobody recorded, and recovery has never been tested end to end.

What the work includes

Design, governance, and the records that go with them.

  • Architecture review of the existing environment
  • Landing zone and governance baseline design
  • Identity, network, and data boundary design
  • Deployment patterns and infrastructure records
  • Backup, recovery, and continuity design
  • Operational runbooks and handover to your team

Deliverables

What you receive.

Architecture reviews

Written findings naming what is at risk, what to change first, and what can wait.

Governance baselines

Subscription structure, identity model, policy, tagging, and cost boundaries, each with a stated reason.

Deployment patterns

A repeatable way to add or change resources that leaves a record of what changed and who approved it.

Operational runbooks

Written procedures for routine operations, failure handling, and recovery, in enough detail to be followed under pressure.

How it supports audit readiness

Every environment decision leaves a trail.

Each environment decision leaves a record an examiner can follow from requirement to running system. Access, change history, and recovery testing are produced by operating the environment rather than assembled before a review.

The same evidence chain is described on audit-ready IT.

What this is not

What this service line does not cover.

  • A managed service. Tectori designs, documents, and hands over. Ongoing operation stays with your team or your provider unless the engagement says otherwise.
  • Vendor resale. Tectori takes no commissions and holds no reseller agreements.
  • Platforms other than Azure by default. Other clouds are considered case by case.
